      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      How does imgLop remove backgrounds for free?

      imgLop runs an AI vision model directly in your browser to separate the main subject from everything behind it, keeping the original resolution intact.

      Are my uploaded photos kept private?

      Yes. Processing happens client-side whenever possible, and your images are never saved to public servers or used to train models.

      Can I edit iPhone HEIC photos?

      Absolutely. HEIC/HEIF files are decoded automatically so you can cut them out and download as PNG or JPG.

      What can the brush tools do?

      After the AI removes the background, open Refine to erase leftover pieces, restore details that were removed by mistake, or use Magic erase to clear enclosed areas such as the inside of an “O”. You can undo and redo every edit.

      Is there a daily limit?

      Free guests get 5 full-resolution credits every day. This limit only applies to AI background removal — PDF conversion and QR generation have no daily limit. Upgrade to imgLop Pro for $4.99/month or $39.99/year for unlimited cutouts. Credits reset at midnight in your time zone, and brush tools, crop and downloads never use credits (and neither do failed removals).

      What does Pro include?

      Pro ($4.99/month, or $39.99/year — about 33% less) includes unlimited cutouts and batches with ZIP download, the erase, restore and Magic erase brush tools, Ultra HD downloads, priority AI processing and an ad-free experience.

      Is the Image-to-PDF converter free and unlimited?

      Yes. Converting images to PDF is free for basic use with no daily limit. Everything is compiled locally in your browser, so your files are never uploaded to our servers.

      Is the QR code generator free and private?

      Yes. Create as many QR codes as you like, pick your own colors and download a PNG. Your text or link is processed 100% in your browser and never sent to our servers.

      Do the PDF and QR tools need an account or software?

      No. They run right in your browser with nothing to install and no sign-up.

      Can I remove backgrounds from many photos at once?

      Yes. Drop up to 40 photos to run a batch. Free users can batch up to their remaining daily credits, and Pro has unlimited batches plus a one-click “Download all as ZIP”. Everything is processed in your browser.

      Do I need an account for Pro?

      Yes. The free tools never need an account, but Pro requires creating one with your email so your subscription is tied to you and works on any device. Your photos, PDFs and QR text still never leave your browser.

      How do I cancel, and what is the refund policy?

      Cancel any time from Account → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Fees are generally non-refundable, but if you were charged in error, contact support within 14 days and we will review it. See the Terms of Service for details.

      Are taxes included in the price?

      Prices exclude applicable taxes such as VAT, GST or sales tax. Where they apply, they are calculated and shown at checkout based on your location.
